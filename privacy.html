Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2026

Applies to Ecto Central (app.ecto.chat)
Applies to Self-Hosted servers
Unmarked — applies to all users

1. Introduction

Ecto ("we", "us", "our") is a chat platform that lets you communicate across servers via text, voice, and video. Ecto operates in two modes:

  • Ecto Central — our hosted service at app.ecto.chat, operated by us.
  • Self-Hosted — instances of the open-source Ecto server software run by independent operators on their own infrastructure.

This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights depending on which mode you use.

2. Information We Collect

All Users

When you create an Ecto account, we collect the information you provide during registration, such as your username, email address, and password (stored as a hash). Messages, files, and other content you send through the platform are transmitted to the servers you connect to.

Ecto Central

In addition to the above, when you use Ecto Central we may collect:

  • IP addresses and approximate geolocation (for security and abuse prevention)
  • Device and browser information (user agent, OS, screen resolution)
  • Usage analytics (features used, session duration, crash reports)
  • Voice/video metadata (call duration, participant count — not call content)

Self-Hosted Servers

We (Ecto) do not collect any data from self-hosted server instances. The operator of a self-hosted server controls what data is collected and stored. Contact your server operator for their privacy practices.

3. How We Use Information

All Users

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Ecto service
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Deliver messages and media to intended recipients

Ecto Central

We additionally use your information to:

  • Improve and optimize the Ecto Central service
  • Detect, prevent, and address abuse, fraud, and security threats
  • Generate aggregated, anonymized analytics to understand usage patterns
  • Send essential service communications (security alerts, policy changes)

4. Data Storage & Security

Ecto Central

Your data on Ecto Central is stored on infrastructure located in the United States. We employ industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Access controls and authentication for internal systems
  • Regular security assessments

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Self-Hosted Servers

Data storage and security for self-hosted instances are entirely the responsibility of the server operator. We provide documentation and best-practice recommendations, but we have no control over how operators configure, secure, or maintain their servers.

5. Data Sharing

All Users

We do not sell your personal data. We do not serve ads. We do not share your data with third-party advertisers.

Ecto Central

We may share limited data with:

  • Infrastructure providers — hosting, CDN, and database services that help us operate Ecto Central, bound by data processing agreements.
  • Legal compliance — when required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Ecto or others.

Self-Hosted Servers

We do not have access to data on self-hosted servers. Any data sharing from a self-hosted instance is determined solely by that server's operator.

6. Your Rights

All Users

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal data
  • Export — receive your data in a portable format

Ecto Central

To exercise these rights on Ecto Central, email privacy@ecto.chat with your request. We will respond within 30 days. You can also delete your account and associated data directly from your account settings.

Self-Hosted Servers

To exercise your data rights on a self-hosted server, contact the server operator directly. We cannot access, modify, or delete data stored on third-party instances.

7. Children's Privacy

Ecto is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us at privacy@ecto.chat and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify users through the Ecto application or by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Ecto after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@ecto.chat
  • GitHub: github.com/ecto-chat
